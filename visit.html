Questions before you come

Can’t see your question here? Send us a message and the team will get back to you.

Ask on WhatsApp
When are you open?

Tuesday to Sunday and on public holidays, from 11am to 9pm. We’re closed on Mondays unless it’s a public holiday.

Who is Tamarims for?

Everyone. There’s soft play and kiddie rides for toddlers and young children, trampoline and arcade games for older kids, and VR, bowling, snooker and massage for teens and adults.

Do I need to book?

Not for a regular visit. Just turn up during opening hours. For birthday parties, school trips and large groups, message us first so we can plan your day.

How do the arcade games work?

Arcade games run on a Tamarims power card that you load at reception. The power card price list is on the wall by the desk, and the team can tell you about current offers.

Can we eat there?

Yes. There’s dine-in seating with food, drinks and snacks, so you can make a full day of it.

Where exactly are you?

Nojeem Ogunlana, off New Road, Awoyaya, Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos. Tap “Get directions” for turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps.
