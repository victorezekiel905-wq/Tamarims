Who we are

A big day out, without the long drive.

Tamarims brings the kind of day out families used to drive to Lekki Phase 1 for right here to Awoyaya. Soft play, trampoline, bowling, VR, arcade games, snooker, a sensory room, massage and an event hall all sit inside one building off New Road.

Little ones get bright, netted play frames built for climbing and sliding. Older kids and teens get something to compete over. Parents get somewhere comfortable to sit, eat and, more often than not, join in.

Reception

Say hello, load your power card and pick up the rules for each play area.

The main play hall

Soft play, the basketball trampoline and the arcade, all on bright green turf.

Bowling alley

Neon lanes, scores on the big screens and shoes at the counter.

VR zone

Space seats, racing rigs, a shooting stand and the 360 motion ride.

Event hall

Round tables and gold chairs for birthdays and get-togethers.

Massage room

A quiet, private room where grown-ups can book a massage and switch off.

The team

Look for the orange and blue shirts.

From the reception desk to the bowling counter, the Tamarims team is on the floor all day. They’ll load your power card, hand out bowling shoes, explain the rules for each play area and help the little ones find their feet.
