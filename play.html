Ten-plus waysto lose an afternoon.

From the ball pit to the bowling lanes, here’s everything waiting for you inside Tamarims.

Soft play and ball pit Basketball trampoline Bowling alley Virtual reality Arcade games Snooker Kiddie rides More
Wave slides into the ball pit

01 / 07

Soft play and ball pit

A multi-level soft play frame in bright red, yellow, green and blue. Children climb, crawl and slide their way round it, and there are tables close by so parents can sit and watch.

  • Wave slides and tube slides
  • A deep ball pit
  • Rope bridges and climbing blocks
  • Seating within sight of the frame
For kids
Hoops at both ends

02 / 07

Basketball trampoline

A fully netted trampoline court with basketball hoops at each end. Bounce high enough and everyone gets to dunk.

  • Netted on every side
  • Two basketball hoops
  • Next to the soft play, easy to keep an eye on
Kids and teens
Scores on the big screen

03 / 07

Bowling alley

Neon-lit lanes with automatic scoring on the screens overhead. Collect your bowling shoes at the counter, pick a ball and play a frame or two.

  • Automatic scoring
  • Bowling shoes at the counter
  • Good for groups, teams and school trips
All ages
It isn’t real. It feels real.

04 / 07

Virtual reality

Pull on a headset and the room disappears. Ride the VR space seats as they tilt and shake, take a racing rig for a spin, step onto the VR shooting stand or strap into the 360 motion ride.

  • VR space travel seats
  • VR racing rigs
  • VR shooting stand
  • 360 motion ride
Teens and adults
Air hockey rematch?

05 / 07

Arcade games

Rows of machines on bright green turf. Race a friend, shoot hoops against the clock, take on the dance machine or settle it once and for all at air hockey.

  • Air hockey and football tables
  • Basketball shooters
  • Racing and shooting cabinets
  • Dance machine and boxing machine
  • Claw machines
All ages
A quieter corner

06 / 07

Snooker

A snooker table in its own room, away from the noise of the main hall. Rack up while the kids are busy on the play floor.

  • Separate room off the main hall
  • Good for teens and adults
Teens and adults
Brrrm brrrm

07 / 07

Kiddie rides

Motorbikes, cars and a spinning ring ride sized for small riders, right beside the arcade.

  • Motorbike and car rides
  • Spinning ring ride
  • Sized for small riders
Little ones

Also inside

Slow down, eat, celebrate.

Not every minute has to be loud. These are for the quieter moments in between.

Sensory room

A calmer room for children who need a quiet moment away from the busy play floor.

Massage

Grown-ups can book a massage and properly switch off while the kids play.

Event hall

Birthdays, school trips and get-togethers in a hall of your own.

Food and drinks

Dine-in seating with meals, drinks, popcorn and slushies.
